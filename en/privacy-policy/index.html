Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit byiskra.com or contact Iskra. It is information about processing, not a request for your consent.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

Iskra is a brand operated by Rino Bago, who is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@byiskra.com.

Contacting Us and Information You Provide

The website has email contact links, not an online contact form. Selecting a contact link opens your email application with our address and a suggested subject. It does not send a message automatically. If you send an email, it is received and stored in Gmail, provided by Google.

We receive your email address, any sender name, the subject and message, attachments you choose to send, and email delivery information. Your message may include your company, website and project requirements. We use this information to read and answer your inquiry and discuss the work you request.

Contacting us is voluntary and is not a legal or contractual requirement for browsing this website. To reply and understand your request, we need a working reply address and enough information about the inquiry. Without these, we may be unable to respond or assess a project. Other details are optional unless we explain why they are needed for a particular next step. Please avoid sending sensitive personal data or unnecessary information about other people.

Purposes and Legal Bases

For steps you ask us to take towards a contract with you personally, such as discussing a requested project or preparing a quote, the legal basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(b). It also applies to processing necessary to perform a contract with you, if one is agreed.

For general correspondence and inquiries from people acting for an organisation, we rely on Article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interest in responding to relevant communications and managing professional relationships. The same basis applies to necessary website delivery and proportionate security measures, and to retaining information needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. These interests must be balanced against your rights; they do not authorise unnecessary processing.

We process information necessary to handle GDPR rights requests under Article 6(1)(c), to meet our obligations under the GDPR. Any further statutory record-keeping obligation must relate to the particular activity and applicable law.

We do not rely on your consent merely because you visit this website, read this policy or email an inquiry. If optional processing requiring consent is introduced, we must explain it and request a separate, informed choice before it starts. Its legal basis would be Article 6(1)(a).

Website Delivery and Service Providers

GitHub Pages hosts this website. To deliver pages, the hosting infrastructure receives technical request information, including your IP address and the requested resource. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing.

The website also requests scripts and styles from cdn.jsdelivr.net, including the Silktide consent-manager files and GSAP animation libraries. These requests occur when the page loads, before any preference choice. The delivery service receives your IP address and request information; browser headers can include browser details and referrer information. These are external file-delivery requests, not evidence of an analytics service. See jsDelivr's privacy information.

Google provides the email service used to receive and reply to inquiries. Message content, attachments and delivery information pass through that service. Namecheap is the domain registrar; domain registration alone does not mean that inquiry messages are sent to Namecheap.

Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities where disclosure is legally required, or to professional advisers where necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Such disclosure must be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

Cookies, Browser Storage and Analytics

We do not use analytics or advertising tracking tools on this website. The existing preference configuration includes an “Analytics” option, but no analytics tracker is connected to it. Selecting that option does not authorise an unspecified future tracker.

The site is configured to load Silktide Consent Manager. Its documented purpose here is to remember preference choices in browser local storage, rather than tracking cookies.

Where storage is strictly necessary to remember a preference you request, the device-storage consent exemption may apply. This is separate from the GDPR basis for any personal-data processing. Other non-essential cookies or similar storage require prior information and consent under the applicable electronic-communications rules.

If consent-based processing is introduced, you must be able to refuse it and withdraw consent as easily as you gave it, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. You can contact us at hello@byiskra.com about any consent you have given us. You can also remove this site's stored browser data through your browser settings; this may reset saved preferences. Clearing browser storage does not erase email correspondence or provider logs.

International Data Transfers

GitHub's privacy statement says it processes personal data in the United States and other countries and generally uses European Commission standard contractual clauses for transfers to countries without an adequacy decision. It also describes its participation in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's transfer information.

GitHub, Google and the delivery infrastructure used for external files operate internationally. Personal data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. The countries and transfer arrangements depend on the particular service and account terms.

Where a transfer falls under the GDPR's international-transfer rules, it requires an applicable legal mechanism, such as a European Commission adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards under Article 46, together with any necessary additional measures. We cannot treat use of this website as consent to an international transfer. Contact hello@byiskra.com to request information about transfers and a copy of any applicable safeguards.

How Long Information Is Kept

For an inquiry that does not lead to a project, the retention criterion is completion of the correspondence and any follow-up you requested. After that, the correspondence should be deleted unless specific information is still needed for a legal obligation or an actual or reasonably anticipated legal claim.

If a project is agreed, relevant correspondence is needed during the project. Any further retention must be tied to the applicable statutory record-keeping period or the relevant legal-claim limitation period. A dispute may require relevant records to be retained until it is resolved. Unrelated information should not be kept simply because other records must be retained.

Technical logs and email backups depend on the providers' settings and retention rules. Browser preference storage is separate from those records and can be removed in your browser. Removing it does not require the providers to delete their logs.

Your Data Protection Rights

You may ask for access to your personal data and a copy, correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, erasure where the GDPR permits it, and restriction of processing in the circumstances set out in the GDPR. Erasure may be limited where information is required by law or for legal claims.

You have the right to object, for reasons relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. That processing must stop unless there are compelling legitimate grounds overriding your interests, rights and freedoms, or it is needed for legal claims. If your data is used for direct marketing, you may object at any time and that marketing must stop.

Where automated processing is based on consent or a contract with you, you may receive personal data you supplied in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and transfer it to another controller. You may also request direct transfer where technically feasible. This portability right does not apply to all records or processing bases.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not make processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal unlawful.

To exercise your rights, email hello@byiskra.com. Requests are normally free. We may request proportionate additional information if there are reasonable doubts about your identity. We must respond without undue delay and within one month of receiving your request. For complex or numerous requests, this may be extended by up to two further months, with an explanation within the first month. If a request is refused, we must explain why and tell you about complaint and judicial remedies.

You may complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka, AZOP): AZOP complaint procedure, azop@azop.hr, Ulica Metela Ožegovića 16, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia. You may also complain to a supervisory authority in the EU/EEA country of your habitual residence, place of work or alleged infringement. You do not have to contact us first to exercise your right to complain. Judicial remedies remain available.

Other People's Data and Automated Decisions

If someone includes another person's contact details in an inquiry, we use only the information necessary to handle that inquiry. Where GDPR Article 14 applies, that person must receive information about the data categories, source, purposes and other required details within one month at the latest, or at the first communication or disclosure if earlier, unless a lawful exception applies.

This website does not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects on visitors.

External Links and Policy Changes

Following a link to another website takes you to a service with its own privacy information. Please review that information before providing personal data there. This does not remove our responsibility for processing for which we are responsible.

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes. If we intend to use personal data for a new purpose, we must provide the required information before doing so and obtain consent where required. Publishing a revised policy does not itself provide consent.
